Embark on an incredible 6-day safari expedition across Kenya's most legendary wilderness regions with **Kenya Tanzania Safari Adventures**. Starting from the coastal shores of Mombasa or Diani and concluding in the vibrant capital of Nairobi, this midrange safari curated by **Nature Romp Safaris** connects the red-dust plains of Tsavo East, the elephant-rich marshlands of Amboseli, the active landscapes of Lake Naivasha, and the predator-dense savannahs of the Maasai Mara.

Enjoy expert-led game drives, spectacular scenic drives through the Great Rift Valley, and stay in comfortable mid-range lodges and tented camps that place you in the heart of Africa's wild action.

### Safari Highlights
* **Seamless point-to-point journey:** Convenient pickup in Mombasa/Diani and drop-off in Nairobi.
* **Tsavo East red elephants:** Explore Kenya's largest park, famous for its red-dust bathing giants.
* **Amboseli's majestic views:** See massive elephant herds grazing in front of Mt. Kilimanjaro.
* **Lake Naivasha & Hell's Gate:** Walk, cycle, or hike amongst volcanic cliffs, geothermal valleys, and diverse wildlife.
* **Maasai Mara big cats:** Witness the unmatched predator action and rich wildlife of Africa's crown jewel.

### Detailed Itinerary

#### Day 1: Mombasa – Tsavo East National Park
Your safari adventure with **Nature Romp Safaris** kicks off with a morning pick-up from your Mombasa hotel or Moi International Airport, followed by a scenic drive inland to Tsavo East National Park, one of Kenya’s oldest and largest conservation areas. The journey unfolds across changing landscapes, from coastal plains to the wild heart of Tsavo.

* **First Game Drive:** As you enter the park, your first game drive begins en route to the lodge, where wildlife sightings may include dust-red elephants, zebras, buffaloes, lions, and lesser kudus. Tsavo East is famed for its "Man-Eaters of Tsavo" legend and is home to over 500 bird species.
* **Afternoon Exploration:** Arrive in time for lunch at the iconic **Voi Safari Lodge**, perched atop a cliff with sweeping views of the wilderness. Later in the afternoon, embark on another game drive across the open plains as the sun sets.
* **Overnight:** Voi Safari Lodge (Tsavo East).

#### Day 2: Tsavo East – Amboseli National Park
Rise early for breakfast before checking out. Enjoy a final morning game drive through Tsavo East as you exit the park and begin your journey westward to Amboseli National Park, located at the base of the majestic Mount Kilimanjaro.

* **Arrival & Lunch:** Arrive around midday and proceed to **Manjaro Tented Camp** (or AA Amboseli Lodge), where you’ll check in and enjoy a warm lunch.
* **Afternoon Game Drive:** After a short rest, head out for a late afternoon game drive, where you’ll encounter large herds of elephants, buffalos, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras, and possibly even elusive predators like lions or leopards.
* ** Kilimanjaro Backdrop:** Enjoy the photogenic scenery as Kilimanjaro’s snowcapped peak emerges from the clouds.
* **Overnight:** Manjaro Tented Camp / AA Amboseli Lodge.

#### Day 3: Amboseli – Lake Naivasha
After an early breakfast, check out and enjoy a final morning game drive as you depart Amboseli. The journey takes you north through the Great Rift Valley with **Kenya Tanzania Safari Adventures**, where a stop at a scenic viewpoint offers sweeping views of ancient escarpments and volcanic terrain.

* **Arrival & Hell's Gate Adventure:** Arrive in Lake Naivasha in time for lunch at **Taphe Guest Resort** (or The Pelican Lodge). In the afternoon, head to Hell’s Gate National Park for an active twist to your safari — choose to explore the dramatic landscape on foot, bike, or even rock climb among cliffs, gorges, and geothermal features.
* **Geothermal Visit:** Visit the nearby Olkaria Geothermal Power Plant to learn about the region’s volcanic energy.
* **Overnight:** Taphe Guest Resort / The Pelican Lodge.

#### Day 4: Lake Naivasha – Maasai Mara National Reserve
Fuel up with breakfast before setting off southwest to the legendary Maasai Mara, a journey that takes you through lush farmlands and rolling savannahs.

* **First Mara Game Drive:** Arrive at **Enchoro Wildlife Camp** in time for lunch and a short rest. In the late afternoon, venture into the Mara plains for your first game drive in this iconic reserve, home to lions, elephants, cheetahs, buffalo, and many other species.
* **Overnight:** Enchoro Wildlife Camp / Ol Moran Tented Camp.

#### Day 5: Full Day Safari in Maasai Mara
Rise with the sun for a morning game drive, when the bush is most alive and predators are likely on the move. Return for breakfast, then set out for a full-day safari with packed lunch to maximize your time in the wild.

* **The Big Five & Great Migration:** Today, track the Big Five—lion, leopard, elephant, rhino, and buffalo—alongside herds of wildebeest, zebras, gazelles, and giraffes. If your visit falls between July and October, you may witness the breathtaking wildebeest migration across the Mara River, where crocodiles lie in wait.
* **Picnic Lunch:** Enjoy your picnic lunch in the heart of the reserve, surrounded by raw nature. Return to camp in the evening.
* **Overnight:** Enchoro Wildlife Camp / Ol Moran Tented Camp.

#### Day 6: Maasai Mara – Nairobi
After your final breakfast in the wild, check out and begin your journey back to Nairobi. Optionally, if you have an evening flight, you can enjoy an extra morning game drive with a packed lunch, departing the Mara around midday.

* **Nairobi Drop-off:** Arrive in Nairobi in the afternoon (between 3:00–6:00 pm). Enjoy optional lunch at the famous Carnivore Restaurant (at your own cost) before your guide drops you at either your hotel or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) for your onward flight.
