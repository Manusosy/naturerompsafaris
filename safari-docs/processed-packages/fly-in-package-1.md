### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
These Q&As can be added under **Step 4: Page Content** in the FAQs accordion.

#### FAQ 1
* **Question:** How do we travel to the Maasai Mara on this Nature Romp Safaris package?
* **Answer:** You will board a scenic bush flight at Wilson Airport in Nairobi and fly directly over the Great Rift Valley to a Maasai Mara airstrip. This exclusive connection by Kenya Tanzania Safari Adventures allows you to avoid the long, bumpy 5-6 hour drive.

#### FAQ 2
* **Question:** What kind of safari vehicles are used for game drives?
* **Answer:** All game drives with Nature Romp Safaris are conducted in custom-designed, private, open-roof 4x4 Safari Land Cruisers. These offer maximum visibility, comfort, and safety with a professional English-speaking guide.

#### FAQ 3
* **Question:** Are optional activities like the hot air balloon ride or Maasai village visit included?
* **Answer:** No, the hot air balloon safari and Maasai cultural village tours are optional activities. They can be added as extras to your Kenya Tanzania Safari Adventures itinerary during your booking or scheduled on-site through your guide.

Your safari adventure with **Nature Romp Safaris** kicks off at Wilson Airport in Nairobi, where you’ll board a scenic bush flight soaring above the Great Rift Valley. Upon landing at the Mara airstrip, your professional **Kenya Tanzania Safari Adventures** guide will greet you and whisk you away in an open-roof 4x4 safari vehicle.
