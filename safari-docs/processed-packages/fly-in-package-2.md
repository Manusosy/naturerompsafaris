#### FAQ 1
* **Question:** What are the flight durations between destinations on this Nature Romp Safaris package?
* **Answer:** This safari maximizes your time in the wild with scenic bush flights. The flight from Nairobi's Wilson Airport to Lewa takes approximately 1 hour 30 minutes, Lewa to Maasai Mara is about 1 hour 25 minutes, and the return flight from the Mara to Nairobi is around 45 to 60 minutes.

#### FAQ 2
* **Question:** What makes Lewa Wildlife Conservancy a highlights destination for Kenya Tanzania Safari Adventures?
* **Answer:** Lewa is a world-renowned conservation success story. It is a critical sanctuary for endangered black rhinos and holds the world’s largest single population of the rare Grevy’s zebra, offering magnificent, crowd-free wildlife viewing.

#### FAQ 3
* **Question:** Are cultural activities like visiting a Maasai village included?
* **Answer:** Yes, this itinerary includes an immersive afternoon visit to a traditional Maasai village on Day 4, where you can learn about their ancient customs, rich history, and vibrant beadwork, and participate in a traditional dance.

### Detailed Itinerary

#### Day 1: Nairobi to Lewa Wildlife Conservancy – Wings Over the Wild
Start your safari adventure with a serene morning in Nairobi followed by a hearty breakfast. You'll then be transferred by **Nature Romp Safaris** to Wilson Airport to board your scheduled bush flight into the untamed beauty of Lewa Wildlife Conservancy. Your aircraft lifts off at 10:20 AM, gliding over the African landscape to touch down around 11:20 AM.

* **Arrival:** Upon landing, your safari guide will welcome you and begin your first game viewing experience en route to **Lewa House**, your lodge nestled in the wilderness.
* **Afternoon Game Drive:** After check-in and a delicious lunch, take time to relax before heading out on your first afternoon game drive, chasing golden-hour sightings until sunset over the savannah.

#### Day 2: Immersive Safari in Lewa Conservancy
Set off before sunrise for an early morning game drive, a prime time to catch predators returning from their nightly hunts. Afterward, return to the lodge for a hearty bush breakfast.

* **Half-Day Expedition:** Later in the day, embark on an extended half-day safari across Lewa's rugged terrain. This Big Five sanctuary is a crucial haven for endangered species like the black rhino and the Grevy’s zebra (holding the world's largest single population). The conservancy also protects ancient forest canopies in Ngare Ndare, home to unique wildlife.
* **Afternoon Walk:** In the afternoon, enjoy an educational guided nature walk with an expert local tracker and unwind with the lodge's premium amenities—whether storytelling by the firepit or sipping sundowners with sweeping views of the northern frontier.

#### Day 3: Lewa to Masai Mara – From Highlands to Iconic Plains
Greet the dawn with one last morning safari in Lewa. After breakfast, transfer to the airstrip for your scheduled flight into the iconic Masai Mara National Reserve, Kenya’s crown jewel of safari experiences.

* **Scenic Flight & Transfer:** Upon landing, your driver-guide will meet you for a scenic game drive transfer to your premium safari camp.
* **Afternoon Safari:** Check-in, indulge in lunch, and soak up the atmosphere before venturing out on a classic afternoon game drive, where lion prides and wildebeest herds await beneath the acacia trees.

#### Day 4: Full-Day Safari Adventure in the Masai Mara
Head out at first light for an early morning game drive, then return to camp for breakfast. The day unfolds with an epic full-day game drive, revealing the rich tapestry of life on the Mara’s open plains.

* **Wildlife Viewing:** Keep your binoculars ready for encounters with the Big Five—elephant, lion, leopard, buffalo, and rhino—as well as massive herds of wildebeest, zebras, and antelopes.
* **Cultural Experience:** In the afternoon, take a break from game driving to visit a traditional Maasai village with **Kenya Tanzania Safari Adventures**, where you’ll learn about ancient customs, beadwork, and join in a spirited traditional dance.
* **Evening:** Return to camp in time to enjoy a beautiful sunset and share stories by the fire.

#### Day 5: Masai Mara to Nairobi – Farewell to the Wild
Rise early for one last dawn safari, bidding farewell to the roaming wildlife that made your journey unforgettable. After a final breakfast at camp, relax before your transfer to the airstrip.

* **Return Flight:** Take to the skies once more as your bush plane returns you to Nairobi’s Wilson Airport (approx. 45–60 mins flight).
* **Transfer:** Upon arrival, you’ll be met and transferred either to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) for your onward journey or to your Nairobi hotel.
