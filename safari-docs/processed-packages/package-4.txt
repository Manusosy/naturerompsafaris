Embark on an incredible 6-day safari expedition across Kenya's most legendary wilderness regions with **Kenya Tanzania Safari Adventures**. Starting from the coastal shores of Mombasa or Diani and concluding in the vibrant capital of Nairobi, this midrange safari curated by **Nature Romp Safaris** connects the red-dust plains of Tsavo East, the elephant-rich marshlands of Amboseli, the active landscapes of Lake Naivasha, and the predator-dense savannahs of the Maasai Mara.

Enjoy expert-led game drives, spectacular scenic drives through the Great Rift Valley, and stay in comfortable mid-range lodges and tented camps that place you in the heart of Africa's wild action.

Tour Highlights
Mombasa to Nairobi route: Point-to-point journey starting on the coast and ending in the capital.
Tsavo East red-dust giants: Encounter the unique red-dust bathing elephants of Kenya's largest national park.
Kilimanjaro backdrop: Spot huge herds of elephants under the snowcapped peak of Africa's highest mountain.
Lake Naivasha boat tour: Enjoy birdwatching and hippo spotting on a fresh water lake, and hiking in Hell's Gate.
Maasai Mara big cats: Unmatched predator-dense plains with lions, leopards, cheetahs, and the Great Migration.

* Frequently Asked Questions:

  FAQ 1
  - Question: How does the transport work for this safari starting in Mombasa and ending in Nairobi?
  - Answer: This safari with Nature Romp Safaris includes a convenient morning pickup from your hotel in Mombasa or Diani, or Moi International Airport. You will travel in a custom safari vehicle (Safari Van or private 4x4 Land Cruiser) and end your tour in Nairobi, with drop-off at your hotel or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport.

  FAQ 2
  - Question: What activities are available at Lake Naivasha and Hell's Gate National Park?
  - Answer: Lake Naivasha offers serene optional boat rides to view hippos and abundant birdlife. At the nearby Hell's Gate National Park, you can enjoy active excursions like walking safaris, cycling among zebras and gazelles, or hiking through gorges and dramatic cliffs.

  FAQ 3
  - Question: Which accommodations are included in this mid-range package?
  - Answer: Kenya Tanzania Safari Adventures handpicks comfortable, quality mid-range lodges and camps, such as Voi Safari Lodge in Tsavo East, AA Amboseli Lodge in Amboseli, Taphe Guest Resort or Pelican Lodge in Naivasha, and Enchoro Wildlife Camp in the Maasai Mara.
